— STRATEGIC LOCATION ANALYSIS

The Yamuna Expressway Growth Axis

The Yamuna Expressway is India’s most rapidly expanding economic corridor. Conceived as a multi-modal transport hub connecting Greater Noida with Agra, the zone is anchored by massive state and national investments:

  • Noida International Airport (Jewar): Located minutes from Quantum 1, creating seamless international cargo, executive flight, and aviation connectivity.
  • YEIDA Industrial & Tech Hubs: Home to upcoming Data Center Parks, Electronic City, Media City, and Olympic Park infrastructure.
  • Multi-Modal Transit Links: Direct connectivity to the Delhi-Mumbai Expressway, Eastern Peripheral Expressway, and proposed Pod Taxi corridors.

— FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

What makes Quantum 1 an institutional-grade business address? +

Quantum 1 is built to Grade-A+ specifications featuring large column-free floor plates, high-speed elevators with destination control, IGBC Platinum-targeted sustainability, and multi-tier security. It is engineered to host global MNC headquarters, tech enterprises, and financial institutions.

Where is Quantum 1 located on the Yamuna Expressway? +

Quantum 1 is strategically situated on the main Yamuna Expressway corridor in Greater Noida, minutes away from the upcoming Noida International Airport (Jewar), Formula 1 Circuit, and the YEIDA Industrial Zone.

Who are the primary architectural and construction partners? +

The architectural design is crafted by Morphogenesis, with civil construction handled by NCC Limited and environmental certification overseen by GBCI.

What property types are available in Quantum 1? +

Quantum 1 offers Grade-A office floors, executive corporate suites, serviced studio apartments, retail spaces, and sky business lounges. Explore serviced spaces here.
