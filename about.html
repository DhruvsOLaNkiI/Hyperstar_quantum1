— ABOUT US

Hyperstar is the developer of Quantum 1, a residential and commercial development in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. With a focus on modern architecture and thoughtful planning, Hyperstar aims to create spaces that support contemporary living, business, and investment.

As a real estate development company in Greater Noida, Hyperstar focuses on quality construction, functional design, strategic connectivity, and long-term value. Our approach goes beyond construction—we aim to create well-planned environments that meet the evolving needs of homeowners, professionals, businesses, and investors.

Quantum 1 by Hyperstar

Quantum 1 offers thoughtfully designed 1BHK and 2BHK residences along with commercial spaces, supported by modern amenities and connectivity within the growing Greater Noida and Yamuna Expressway corridor.

Greater Noida is emerging as an important destination for real estate in Greater Noida, attracting homebuyers, investors, businesses, and property professionals looking for residential and commercial opportunities.

Our Commitment

At Hyperstar, our development approach is built on three core principles: quality construction, thoughtful design, and transparent execution.

From planning and development to project delivery, our focus remains on creating spaces that are modern, functional, connected, and designed around the needs of their users.

Through Quantum 1, Hyperstar aims to create a modern and future-ready destination in Greater Noida.
